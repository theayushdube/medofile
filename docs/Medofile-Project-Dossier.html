01 · Executive summary

What Medofile is

Medofile is a connected healthcare platform that helps people find trusted doctors, make informed booking decisions, and stay organized before and after appointments.

Core product idea: Medofile is not only a doctor booking directory. It connects discovery, booking, reminders, follow-up instructions, care history, verified reviews, health education, and AI-guided next steps in one care journey.

Discover

Search doctors by specialty, city, symptoms, availability, fee, language, consultation type, or current location.

Decide

Compare doctor profiles, experience, fees, ratings, treatments, reviews, and care options.

Continue care

Manage appointments, receive reminders, view timelines, read follow-up instructions, and keep care information organized.

Product goals

  • Make healthcare discovery easier and more local.
  • Give patients more confidence before booking.
  • Help doctors build a trustworthy digital presence.
  • Support patients after an appointment, not only before it.
  • Use AI for guidance and organization while keeping doctors responsible for diagnosis and treatment.

03 · Patient experience

Complete patient workflow

1. DiscoverVisit Medofile or search online.
→
2. SearchUse specialty, city, symptom, or location.
→
3. CompareReview doctor profiles and care options.
→
4. BookChoose date, time, and consultation type.
→
5. ContinueReceive reminders and follow-up care.

1. Discover care

The homepage is optimized for intent such as “doctor near me”, “find a trusted doctor”, “doctor by specialty”, and “book a doctor appointment”. Patients can browse before signing in.

2. Search and location matching

The search screen supports doctor name, specialty, city, language, gender, consultation type, availability, fee, rating, experience, and current location. When location permission is granted, the browser sends coordinates to the API and doctors are filtered and ranked by distance.

3. Compare doctors

A profile presents qualification, experience, specialty, fees, rating, review count, clinic and hospital information, languages, timings, treatments, education, memberships, insurance, branches, social profiles, and booking availability.

4. Book an appointment

The booking endpoint validates the doctor, patient contact details, date, time, and care type. Signed-in bookings are associated with the patient’s Clerk ID. Guest bookings remain supported.

5. Manage appointments

Signed-in patients can see appointment status, cancel eligible upcoming bookings, or reschedule them. Each important action creates a care timeline event so the patient has a record of what changed.

6. Continue after the appointment

Doctors can add follow-up instructions. Patients can see reminders, appointment events, follow-ups, and care history in the care space.

7. Review after completed care

Reviews require a completed appointment with the doctor. Suspicious or inappropriate content can be held for moderation, and published verified reviews update doctor ratings.

04 · Doctor experience

Doctor and practice workflow

Doctor onboarding

  1. Doctor creates a Clerk account.
  2. Doctor opens the registration flow.
  3. Doctor provides professional identity and contact details.
  4. Doctor selects one or more specialties.
  5. Doctor adds clinic, city, timing, fees, and consultation types.
  6. Doctor submits the profile for platform use and potential verification.

Profile presentation

  • Specialty and qualification
  • Experience and ratings
  • Clinic and hospital details
  • Languages and treatments
  • Education and memberships
  • Insurance and branches
  • Availability and booking options
  • Location and social links

Doctor dashboard

The doctor dashboard centralizes booking leads, patient appointments, profile editing, follow-up instructions, and practice insights. Analytics include impressions, profile views, calls, chats, bookings, directions, and weekly trends.

Specialty storage decision

Doctor specialties currently remain in the existing string field as comma-separated values. This preserves backward compatibility with older doctor records while allowing the web registration and profile editor to use a searchable multi-select UI.

Admin authentication

Admin credentials are validated server-side. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed and successful login produces a signed JWT. Protected API routes validate the Bearer token. Superadmin endpoints additionally require the superadmin role.

Admin responsibilities

  • Maintain doctor profile quality and verification state.
  • Review bookings and operational activity.
  • Moderate suspicious or pending reviews.
  • Manage blog settings and generated health articles.
  • Monitor platform analytics and content performance.
  • Manage authorized administrator accounts.

11 · Security and privacy

Security model

Identity separation

Clerk manages patient and doctor identity. Admin users use a separate JWT system. This prevents mixing public accounts with internal administration.

Ownership scoping

Patient bookings, saved doctors, care events, follow-ups, and reminders are associated with the relevant Clerk user ID.

Server-side secrets

OpenAI, Clerk secret, session, JWT, and Resend integration credentials are used server-side and are not shipped to the browser.

AI guardrails

Medical responses avoid definitive diagnosis, dosage advice, and medication changes. Serious symptoms receive emergency guidance.

Review protection

Reviews are tied to completed appointments and validated for length, contact details, links, and suspicious language.

Contact protection

Contact enquiries use input limits, email validation, a honeypot field, rate limiting, and generic delivery errors.

Healthcare boundary: Medofile provides discovery tools, educational information, and AI-assisted guidance. It does not replace a qualified healthcare professional or emergency services.
12 · SEO and growth

Search visibility strategy

Medofile is optimized around doctor discovery intent, especially “doctor near me” searches in India.

On-page SEO

  • Homepage title targets “Doctor Near Me in India”.
  • Homepage H1 includes “Find a trusted doctor near me”.
  • Doctor listing page has a unique title and description.
  • Doctor profiles receive dynamic metadata.
  • Supporting copy explains specialty, city, and location search.

Technical SEO

  • Canonical URLs
  • Open Graph and Twitter metadata
  • Organization and WebSite structured data
  • SearchAction schema
  • robots.txt
  • sitemap.xml
  • Descriptive internal links

Important ranking expectation

SEO implementation makes the site understandable and crawlable, but rankings depend on search-engine crawling, indexing, location relevance, site authority, page quality, competition, and ongoing content publishing. Google Search Console should be used to monitor actual impressions and queries.

15 · End-to-end examples

How common scenarios work

Example A: Patient searches for a cardiologist near Mumbai

  1. Patient opens Medofile and searches for cardiology.
  2. Patient selects Mumbai or allows location access.
  3. Frontend requests doctors with specialization, city, latitude, longitude, and radius.
  4. API filters and sorts relevant doctors.
  5. Doctor cards show ratings, experience, fees, clinic details, and availability.
  6. Patient opens a profile and requests a booking.
  7. API creates the booking and, if signed in, adds the event to the care timeline.

Example B: Patient asks the AI about recurring headaches

  1. Patient sends the symptom through the AI Assistant.
  2. API adds the safety system prompt.
  3. AI describes several possibilities without diagnosing.
  4. AI suggests an appropriate specialist, such as a neurologist or general physician.
  5. AI returns a structured doctor recommendation tag.
  6. Frontend requests matching doctors.
  7. Patient can open a doctor profile and book care.

Example C: Doctor adds post-appointment instructions

  1. Doctor opens a completed patient booking.
  2. Doctor writes follow-up instructions.
  3. API verifies the doctor and booking relationship.
  4. Follow-up is stored against the appointment and patient Clerk ID.
  5. A care timeline event is created.
  6. Patient sees the instruction in the care section.

Example D: Visitor sends a contact enquiry

  1. Visitor completes the contact form.
  2. Frontend sends the form to /api/contact.
  3. API validates fields, checks the honeypot, and applies rate limiting.
  4. Resend delivers the enquiry to admin@digitaldhatu.com.
  5. The visitor receives a success or friendly error message.
